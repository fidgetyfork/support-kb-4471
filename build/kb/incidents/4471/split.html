The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A tenant whose locale writes the day first gets day and month swapped: “Booked for 2 March. The invite says 3 February.” This is the bug #4471 describes, and the queued fix is for it.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type month first. Its date field shows the hint MM/DD/YYYY, so people type the month first, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The dates come out swapped too, so they look like the parser bug, but the parser fix does not touch them. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants land here.

one-day: the date moves by one day. These reports describe no swap at all: “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” The desk already noted them (desk-notes.json), but the cause table files them under parser because of their tenant. A date that shifts by a day points at time zones, not at date order.

Why they were merged

  • Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. All three problems produce the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”), so they all landed in #4471.
  • Intake’s intakeTag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. It often disagrees with the tenant, in both directions, so it cannot be used to split them.
  • The export pages overlap: some reports appear twice. Count by id.

For the desk

  • When a report’s dates are swapped, check the tenant’s form before merging: a booking-v3 form report goes to the hint ticket.
  • When a date moved by one day, it is not a swap. File it on the one-day ticket.
  • Reopen decision 0001 with a new record: subject alone hides different problems.
